About ABB

🏍️ Welcome to ABB – Axom Bikers Brigade. Founded in 2024 in Bangalore, ABB is a passionate biker community built by people from Assam living outside their home state.

  • Unity on wheels
  • Empowering women
  • Helping the needy
  • Support each other
  • Spread happiness
  • Ride for causes
